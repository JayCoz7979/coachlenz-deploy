# CoachLenz — First Outreach Push (run this week)

## The one move
**Free, done-for-you film breakdown.** Not "sign up" — *"Send me one game, I'll break it
down free and send you the report."* Founder-delivered, local-first. It's the
highest-converting cold start, and it fills your gates with REAL activation: a coach who
says yes becomes a signup + a first analysis, which seeds your retention cohort.

Do NOT lead with "try my app." Lead with "I'll do the work for you, free, on one game."

## Target — first 30, local-first
- **Who:** HS head coaches + offensive/defensive coordinators, football and basketball,
  who already film games (Hudl users). They feel the midnight-film-scrubbing pain.
- **Start warm:** Athens / Tanner / Huntsville / North Alabama — your backyard. One or
  two degrees of separation convert far better than cold strangers. Mine your own network
  first (former coaches, school contacts, church, anyone who knows a coach).
- **Where to get contacts:** school athletic-department staff pages (emails are public),
  Hudl team pages, MaxPreps, the AHSAA school directory, and X/Twitter (many HS coaches
  are active and take DMs).
- **Build a simple list** (one row per coach):

| Name | School | Sport | Email / DM | Warm? | Sent | Replied | Said yes |
|------|--------|-------|------------|-------|------|---------|----------|

## Follow-up (one bump, 3–4 days later, same thread)
> Coach, still glad to break down a game for you free — takes you two minutes to send a
> link. Want me to?

Then stop. One bump, not five.

## Send plan + discipline
- **Batch of 25–30.** Personalize the first line (their school, record, a recent game).
  Send from your real email, one at a time — not a blast tool. It's a founder's note;
  deliverability and tone both matter.
- **Measure, don't scale.** Watch Admin → Funnel after the batch:
  - Conversion (visitor → signup): **PASS ≥ 5%, STOP < 2%.**
  - The real signal at this stage: how many replies turn into "they sent me a game."
- **One channel at a time.** Prove local coach outreach before you touch SEO or ads. A
  leaky funnel scaled just loses faster.
- **Batch-1 win condition:** 2–3 coaches say yes to a free breakdown. That's your first
  activation and the seed of your retention cohort — the number that unblocks everything.
