# Parents' Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security

> **⚠️ DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED. Not legal advice.** Intended to be publicly accessible without login (New York Education Law §2-d).

**Provider:** Cosby AI Solutions LLC ("CoachLenz") · **Effective date:** `[FILL-IN]`

CoachLenz supports each educational agency's obligations under New York Education Law §2-d. Parents, legal guardians, and eligible students have the following rights regarding student personally identifiable information (PII):

1. **No sale or commercial release.** A student's PII will **not be sold** or released for any **commercial or marketing purpose**.

2. **Right to inspect, review, and correct.** Parents have the right to inspect and review the complete contents of their child's education record maintained through CoachLenz, and to request correction of inaccuracies, by submitting a request to their school; the school may contact CoachLenz, which will make the data available to the school.

3. **Right to opt out of disclosure.** Parents may opt out of the disclosure of directory information and may direct, through their school, how such information is handled.

4. **Security and encryption.** Student PII is protected by safeguards including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and other administrative, technical, and physical measures aligned to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. State and federal law safeguard PII while stored or in transit.

5. **Right to file a complaint.** Complaints about possible breaches or unauthorized release of student PII may be submitted to the student's school, or to the New York State Education Department:
   Chief Privacy Officer, New York State Education Department, 89 Washington Avenue, Albany, NY 12234 · CPO@nysed.gov · (as published by NYSED).

6. **Third parties.** Where CoachLenz uses sub-processors (listed in the CoachLenz Privacy Policy) to deliver the service, those parties are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and student PII is used only for the authorized educational purpose.

7. **Contact — CoachLenz privacy contact.** privacy@coachlenz.com · Cosby AI Solutions LLC.

8. **More information.** See the CoachLenz Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.md`) and Data Privacy Agreement (`data-privacy-agreement.md`) for the full description of data collected, its use, retention, and deletion.

*This Bill of Rights supplements, and does not replace, the Parents' Bill of Rights published by the student's educational agency.*
