This policy explains how coachlenz.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

## 1. Essential cookies (no consent required)
Used to keep you signed in and secure your session (authentication tokens, session state, CSRF protection). The Service will not function without these.

## 2. Analytics cookies
CoachLenz does **not** use third-party analytics cookies (no Google Analytics, PostHog, or similar). We rely only on our own server logs to operate and secure the Service. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, for EU/UK visitors, request consent before setting any non-essential analytics cookie.

## 3. No advertising cookies
CoachLenz does **not** use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and does not use student data for advertising.

## 4. How to control cookies
You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and (where offered) decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in.
